I'm Bishnu, a computer science student based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I love digging into how things work at a fundamental level — from file systems and storage internals to the algorithms that power everyday software.

My curiosity drives me to explore topics like ext4, NTFS, journaling, memory allocation, and OS design. When I'm not studying CS fundamentals, I'm building projects that bridge theory with practice.

I'm looking for opportunities to work on meaningful systems, contribute to open source, and keep learning from great engineers.
